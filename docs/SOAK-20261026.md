# Extended headless observation, 26 October 2026

The frozen 0.1.0-alpha.1 package completed four hours on NVIDIA UHD30 production and Intel HD30 standby. NVIDIA passed the bounded UHD30 observation. Intel remains accepted for its limited camera/map workload with a recorded performance exception; this run does not establish uninterrupted native 30 fps on that hardware.

Both engines rendered without a desktop session, desktop socket mounts or display environment variables. Existing rundowns and music remained active, with normal watchdog recovery enabled. No application image or publishing route changed.

At 18:40:05 UTC, standby reported 6.39 fps while its on-air camera's decoded picture was 10.692 seconds old. The preceding camera sample reported 30.00 fps and the next, on a map scene, reported 29.98 fps. Container start times were unchanged. The exact cause remains unresolved; correlation does not establish whether network, source recovery or rendering work caused the dip.

## Source continuity

Production had 13 observations containing a stale source: three for NTV camera 1 and ten for NTV camera 4. Each was ready again at the next observation. The other four enabled sources had no sampled stale pictures.

Standby had 46 observations containing one or more stale sources. Per-source counts were four for camera 1, three for camera 2, 38 for NTV camera 1 and three for NTV camera 2. Simultaneous events make these counts sum to more than 46. All recorded source episodes recovered before the end. Native camera-readiness warnings occurred more often than decoded-source failures and are retained separately.

These are sampled events, not exact outage durations. No source event caused a sampled programme stall or watchdog restart. Repeated standby NTV camera 1 interruptions remain a source-continuity follow-up.

## Independent receiver checks

All six scheduled 90-second recordings, one per station near the start, middle and end, passed full decoding. Each measured 30.000 video packets per second, with non-silent 48 kHz audio, no large video/audio timestamp gaps and acceptable stream-start/end coverage.

Representative frames were inspected from every recording. Production showed weather, a quad with four live camera pictures and the marine forecast. Standby showed a live camera and both airspace maps. Graphics, lower thirds, ticker and clock were aligned in those frames, with no observed downward smear.

The six clips total nine minutes of recorded coverage across two four-hour observations. They are not continuous four-hour recordings, a perceptual lip-sync measurement or a YouTube-client listening test.

## Resources and restoration

The NVIDIA GPU reached 54 C, with reported GPU memory between 1,442 and 2,384 MiB. Engine container memory increased from approximately 1.13 to 1.52 GiB, with a 1.65 GiB peak. Intel CPU temperature reached 79 C; its engine container memory ranged from approximately 1.66 to 2.34 GiB and ended at 1.81 GiB. No OOM event or service replacement was recorded.

Free disk space changed by less than 4 MiB on either station despite cumulative write counters advancing. This supports bounded retained storage during this run, not an attribution of individual writes. The NVIDIA memory increase merits longer-duration tracking; four hours cannot establish the absence of a slow leak.

Production automatically restored desktop output at 22:29:02 UTC, approximately 42 seconds after observation ended. The complete station configuration compared equal to its pre-test snapshot. UHD30, HDMI and desktop audio were enabled; the graphical boot target, original lingering policy, rundown, watchdog and display helper were restored. The maintenance marker was absent and the timed recovery backstop was cancelled.

A subsequent USB HDMI capture showed the programme map, aircraft, ticker and clock, with a non-silent low-level audio signal. USB buffering and the uncalibrated capture path are not cadence, colour or speaker-volume acceptance. Native programme audio and advancing UHD30 output were also verified after restoration.

A separate 90-second production receiver recording after restoration passed full decoding, 30.000 video packets per second, non-silent 48 kHz audio, stream coverage and timestamp-gap checks. The running engine and controller image identities still matched the frozen installer.

## Read-only transport comparison

Three paired 30-second joins at the basement relay passed full decoding, stream coverage and packet cadence for both RTMP and RTSP. RTMP measured 30.000 video packets per second; RTSP ranged from 29.988 to 30.003. No large audio gaps were found.

These reads used the forwarded standby stream at the basement relay. They did not exercise the original laptop relay endpoint that previously delayed RTSP audio by 3.056 seconds. The earlier defect remains open, and publishing continues through RTMP. [MediaMTX issue 6300](https://github.com/bluenviron/mediamtx/issues/6300) remained open when checked on 26 October; its timing mechanism is a lead, not an established cause of the local failure.

## Acceptance limits

This closes the extended NVIDIA headless UHD30 observation and adds four hours of Intel HD30 continuity evidence with the recorded exception. It does not qualify UHD60, automatic Intel quad rotation, indefinite source availability or an unrestricted GPU matrix.

Physical power-off/on recovery and a clean supported-GPU installation are separate requirements from this observation. The subsequent [controlled power-cycle record](POWER-CYCLE-20261026.md) covers NVIDIA desktop startup; clean GPU installation remains open.
