About the charts

Readings are checked every five minutes. History begins when this station starts collecting it; gaps are left visible. These are different system measures and must not be added together. Island demand excludes Labrador and export commitments.

The station keeps up to 90 days locally. A missing or old reading is labelled rather than treated as zero.

Report data and import status

Daily peaks and forecasts come from printed report values. Intraday curves are approximate readings from PDF vector graphs. Report dates, filing dates, original PDFs and revisions are retained. These reports do not supply historical Muskrat Falls or Churchill Falls generation. Forecasts remain labelled by the date they were issued.

The importer checks for new reports every six hours and gradually imports the past 90 days. Reports that fail validation remain listed below; they are not silently substituted.
